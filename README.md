# Training Materials

Training curricula developed for National Statistics Office staff and
survey practitioners across USAID Feed the Future focus countries,
produced under the USAID Surveys in Monitoring Resilience and Food
Security (SMRFS) project (ICF, 2022–2025).

Materials cover four topic areas: Introduction to GIS, GIS for Managers,
Small Area Estimation Methods, and Advanced Data Analysis in R. Audiences ranged from field enumerators
to senior government statisticians, local NGO staff, and trainings were delivered in Ghana,
Uganda, and Nepal in coordination with national statistics offices and
USAID mission staff.
